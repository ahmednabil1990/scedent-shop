How PALM builds your model

Three captures, one accurate digital model for your lab.

  1. Capture implant positions

    PALM photographs the coded scan bodies and solves every implant's position in about ten seconds.

  2. Capture soft tissue

    Scan the gums with your intraoral scanner as usual; no full-arch stitching of implant positions needed.

  3. Merge into one model

    Positions and tissue combine into a precise digital model, ready for a passive-fit framework.
